### **FOR STUDENTS:**

1. Open `MASTER_NAVIGATION_START_HERE.html`

4. Click "Start Chapter 1" → Begin learning

#### **Day 1: Learning Episode 1**
1. Read Layer 1 (Definition) - understand WHAT it is

4. **NEW: Question Layer 4 (Assumptions)** - challenge beliefs
5. **NEW: Explore Layer 5 (Connections)** - see 6 domains
6. **NEW: Solve Layer 6 (Applications)** - Harvard case
7. **NEW: Reflect Layer 7 (Implications)** - Oxford essay

1. Click "Tutorial Defense" → 3-5 rounds of Socratic questioning

#### **Daily: Check Dashboard**
1. Open Dashboard → See today's growth
2. Check "Today's Focus" → Know what to practice
3. See breakthroughs → Celebrate progress
