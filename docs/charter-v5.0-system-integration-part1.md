### **LAYER 2: MECHANISM (How Does It Work?)**

**Cricket Example:**

**Traditional:**
```
"Current flows when potential difference is applied."
```

**Our System:**
```
AI: "Let's dig deeper. HOW do charges start flowing?

Think about cricket: What makes the ball spin?
Student: "Bowler's hand gives it energy!"

Exactly! The hand PUSHES the ball into rotation.

In a wire, what pushes electrons?
Student: "Um... the battery?"

YES! Battery creates an ELECTRIC FIELD.
This field PUSHES electrons (like hand pushes ball).

But electrons don't go straight! They bump into atoms,
like ball bumping into air molecules.

MECHANISM UNLOCKED:
1. Battery creates electric field (push force)
2. Field pushes electrons (they accelerate)
3. Electrons collide with atoms (slow down)
4. Net result: Slow steady drift (drift velocity)

This drift = current we measure!
```

**Layer 2 Unlocked:**
- ✅ Student understands MECHANISM
- ✅ Not just "what" but "how"
- ✅ Causal chain clear

---

### **LAYER 3: FORMULA (Precise Quantification)**

**Cricket Example:**

**Traditional:**
```
"I = Q/t where I is current, Q is charge, t is time."
```

**Our System:**
```
AI: "Now let's make this PRECISE.

If Chahal's ball creates X amount of air flow per second,
how do we MEASURE that?

Student: "Count how many air molecules pass?"

Exactly! 

For current, we count charges:
- If 10 coulombs pass in 5 seconds
- Flow rate = 10/5 = 2 coulombs per second
- This is 2 Amperes!

FORMULA: I = Q/t

But here's the deeper formula (Grade 12):
I = n × e × A × vd

Let me show you how Grade 9 formula GROWS into Grade 12...

Q (total charge) = How many charges?
Answer: (n × A × vd × t) × e

Where:
- n = number density (charges per volume)
- A = area
- vd = drift velocity (like ball's movement)
- t = time
- e = charge per electron

Substitute into I = Q/t:
I = (n × A × vd × t × e) / t
I = n × e × A × vd

FORMULA UNLOCKED: Grade 9 → Grade 12!
```

**Layer 3 Unlocked:**
- ✅ Student knows FORMULA
- ✅ Understands where it comes from (not magic equation)
- ✅ Can connect basic → advanced formulas
