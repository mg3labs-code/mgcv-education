### The Oxford Method Digitized

**What It Shows:**
The tutorial system is Oxford's secret weapon—students defend their reasoning verbally under intellectual pressure. We've made this scalable through AI.

**Demo Flow:**
1. Student gives initial answer to concept question ("Why does ice float?")

3. Student must defend reasoning via voice (or text in demo)
