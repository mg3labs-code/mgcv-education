### **PHASE 4: OXFORD METHODS (Optional, Recommended)**

#### **Option A: Tutorial Defense (5 min)**

```
AI: "You learned Division Algorithm. Explain it to me 
     as if I'm a 5th grader."

Student: "Division Algorithm means when you divide 
          one number by another, you get a quotient 
          and remainder..."

AI: "Good start. But you said 'divide'. What does 
     'divide' actually mean at a fundamental level?"

Student: [Thinks deeper, refines answer]

AI: "Better! Now challenge: Can remainder ever equal 
     the divisor? Defend your answer."

Student: [Argues their position]

AI: [3-5 rounds of Socratic questioning]

Final Score: Argumentative Capacity: 78%
    ↓
Inner OS Updated:
    Critical Thinking: +3%
    Mental Clarity: +2%
```

#### **Option B: First Principles (10 min)**

```
5-Step Protocol:

STRIP: "Explain Division Algorithm in simplest words"
    → Student writes simple explanation

ASSUMPTIONS: "What are you taking for granted?"
    → Student lists: integers exist, division works...

REBUILD: "Build it from scratch"
    → Student reconstructs: numbers → counting → 
      grouping → division → remainder concept

TEST: "What if remainder could be negative?"
    → Student predicts consequences

APPLY: "Use this in a new context"
    → Student applies to time calculations

Score: 82% (Deep Understanding)
    ↓
Inner OS Updated:
    Mental Clarity: +5%
    Critical Thinking: +4%
```
