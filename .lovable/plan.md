# MGCV whole-product demo and video

## What you will get
- A public, no-login MGCV demonstration inspired by the guided HUMAIN website, without copying its school-finance features.
- A matching approximately 90-second, landscape English video: generated classroom scenes plus accurate MGCV screen walkthroughs.
- Existing student and teacher pages remain unchanged.

## Clickable demo website
Create a dedicated `/showcase` page with MGCV prominently visible and a persistent **Fictional demo data** label.

1. **Choose a journey:** Student, Teacher, or School leadership.
2. **Student:** select an interest, connect it to a Class 9 concept, explore the seven learning layers, answer a sample question and explain the reasoning. Include a sample calendar and homework upload/review story.
3. **Teacher:** explore a subject-specific teaching plan, adjust a sample lesson date, review five-question homework, preview publishing, and review a sample student's answer before finalizing marks.
4. **School leadership:** follow a fictional student's learning evidence from classroom activity to teacher review. Avoid invented growth percentages, predictive accuracy or school-wide performance claims.
5. **Watch the demo:** include the finished video with playback controls and a downloadable copy.

Interactions use browser-only fictional examples and reset cleanly. No login, live school records, account creation, AI marking calls or changes to production plans.

## Accuracy and safety
- Preserve CBSE Science with Physics, Chemistry and Biology teaching sections; don't delete subject content.
- Clearly separate fictional demonstrations from verified working features and prototypes.
- Pilot readiness stays pending; the isolated 40-student test remains not tested, and physical-device testing remains waived/not tested.
- Do not connect the public demo to live school data or present it as the separate load-test environment.
- No repeated Golden Journeys, real-AI homework generation, password resets, data cleanup or publishing.

## Technical approach
- React page and focused demo components, using existing MGCV design tokens, controls and reusable learning assets.
- Isolated local demo state; no changes to access policies, homework generation or existing protected routes.
- Generate short contextual clips, then compose the full screen-led walkthrough and save its downloadable MP4 in Files; embed through the project's asset flow.
- Check desktop and narrow-screen layouts, each demo journey, reset behavior and video playback. Inspect video scenes and transitions before delivery.
