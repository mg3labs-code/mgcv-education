Layer 1

What it means

Nutrition is how living things get food and use it for energy, growth, repair, and health.

💡 Word meaning: Nutrition means taking in and using food.
📅 Scientist: Many biologists and doctors built our understanding of food and health.
⭐ Why it matters: Nutrition explains how organisms survive and why balanced food matters.
🌿
Life lesson: Your body and your mind both need the right inputs. What you take in shapes what you can do.

Step 1: Organisms take in food or make food.

Step 2: Food is broken down and used for energy, growth, and repair.

Layer 4

Hidden ideas

Some ideas are hidden inside every explanation. Good students learn to notice them.

🔍 Hidden idea: Food is needed only for energy → Check: food also helps growth and repair.
🔍 Hidden idea: All organisms eat in the same way → Check: plants and animals get food differently.
🔍 Hidden idea: More food always means better health → Check: balance matters.

Layer 6

How we use it

🌱 Farming: More carbon dioxide in greenhouses can help some crops grow better.
⚡ Clean energy: Scientists are trying to copy photosynthesis to make clean fuel.
🏭 Factories: Algae can help remove carbon dioxide from factory air.
🚀 Space travel: Plants may help astronauts get food and oxygen.
