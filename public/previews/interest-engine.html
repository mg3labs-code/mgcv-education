How the interest engine works behind the scenes
1
Student picks interest — one-time, on first login. Stored as their profile signal. Cricket, food, gaming, music etc. — 8 categories, expandable.
2
Every concept has 8 hook variants — one per interest. The textbook concept doesn't change. The entry point does. Same integers lesson, 8 different ways in.
3
The hook is written before content — not after. Interest → real-world scenario → textbook logic emerges naturally. The curriculum becomes the explanation, not the starting point.
4
Student can change interest anytime — no label, no track. A student who says cricket today can switch to music next week. The system adapts. No judgement.
5
Interest data becomes insight — teachers see which triggers got the best first-thought responses. This tells you which real-world frames land for this class. That's the behavioral moat.
